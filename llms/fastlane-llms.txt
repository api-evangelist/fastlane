# FastLane Labs

> FastLane Labs is a blockchain infrastructure provider building maximal extractable value (MEV) and liquid staking products on the Monad blockchain. It operates a public, on-chain MEV auction that a majority of Monad validators route blocks through, and maintains Atlas, an open-source execution-abstraction framework. Developer surfaces are JSON-RPC APIs and Solidity smart contracts rather than a REST/OpenAPI catalog.

## Products
- [shMON — liquid staking token](https://shmonad.xyz): Earns staking rewards plus revenue from FastLane's MEV protocol.
- [Smart Vault — USDC yield](https://www.ligomez.com/): USDC-denominated staking-yield exposure without holding MON.
- [hMON — yield donation](https://shmonad.xyz/degen): Yield-donation product.
